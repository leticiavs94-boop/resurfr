06 — Guidelines

Do's & Don'ts

Keep the brand consistent by following these rules.

Do

  • Use the yellow (#C8E62B) for primary CTAs
  • Keep Barlow Condensed for headlines only
  • Use DM Sans for all body and UI text
  • Maintain 8px spacing increments
  • Use the green sidebar in the app
  • Keep border-radius between 8–24px

Don't

  • Use Barlow Condensed for body paragraphs
  • Place the yellow logo on white backgrounds without the icon box
  • Mix in off-brand colors for status badges
  • Use sharp corners (0px radius) on cards
  • Stretch or distort the logo mark
  • Use font weights below 600 for Barlow
